# FUL.HOUSE — Real Pets as Digital Companions

## Core idea

Users should be able to digitize their real pets instead of being limited to choosing a generic virtual cat, dog, parrot or fantasy pet.

**Upload your real pet. Give them a home online.**

The user uploads photos and, ideally, short videos of their real pet. FUL.HOUSE analyzes the animal's visual identity and creates a recognizable digital version that lives inside the owner's house.

This should become the preferred pet experience. Generic starter pets can remain available for users who do not have a real animal or do not want to upload one.

---

## Why this matters

A generic digital pet is decoration.

A digital version of the user's actual dog, cat, bird or other companion creates emotional ownership immediately.

The house is supposed to be built from the owner's real digital life. Pets are part of that life and therefore should be represented in the same way as work, photographs, music, projects and memories.

The principle is consistent with the rest of FUL.HOUSE:

**Do not ask the user to construct an artificial identity when their real identity can generate the experience.**

---

## Creation flow

### Minimum input

- Several photos from different angles.
- Pet name.
- Species/type.

### Better input

- 5–20 photos.
- One or more short videos showing movement.
- Optional voice/audio samples for pets with recognizable sounds.
- Age.
- Breed if known.
- Sex if the owner wants to provide it.
- Short personality description.

Examples:

- Calm
- Very active
- Sleeps everywhere
- Loves strangers
- Afraid of vacuum cleaners
- Steals food
- Always waits near the door

The AI combines visual evidence with owner-provided personality information.

---

## Generated digital pet

The generated pet should preserve recognizable characteristics where technically possible:

- Coat color and markings
- Face shape
- Eye color
- Ear shape
- Tail
- Body proportions
- Distinctive marks
- Collar/accessories
- Typical posture
- Characteristic movement derived from video when feasible

The goal is not photorealism. The goal is recognition:

> That's my dog.

The pet should be adapted to the visual style of the owner's house while remaining identifiable.

---

## Personality and behavior

The digital pet should not be a static 3D asset.

It can develop a lightweight behavioral profile based on:

1. Owner description.
2. Uploaded videos.
3. Explicit corrections from the owner.
4. Interactions inside FUL.HOUSE.

Examples:

- Sleeps in particular rooms.
- Waits at the door when the owner has been away.
- Runs toward the owner on return.
- Hides from visitors.
- Approaches visitors.
- Plays with certain objects.
- Has a favorite chair.
- Follows the Butler.
- Occasionally causes harmless house events.

The system should not pretend to infer an animal's real psychology from a few images. Personality is primarily owner-authored and interaction-driven.

---

## Butler integration

The AI Butler knows the pet's name and approved history.

Examples:

> Charlie has been sleeping in your office all morning.

> While you were away, Luna knocked over the plant again.

> Your pet waited near the entrance when you returned.

The Butler can help configure behavior, memories and privacy.

---

## Real pet + connected memories

With explicit permission, FUL.HOUSE can associate the pet with selected photographs and videos from connected photo services or manually uploaded archives.

This can create:

- Pet photo albums
- Birthday/adoption memories
- Favorite places
- Timeline of the pet's life
- Objects associated with the pet
- Historical versions as the pet ages

Nothing from private photo libraries becomes public automatically.

---

## Aging

A digital pet may visually evolve as the real pet ages if the owner periodically adds new photographs or video.

Possible flow:

New media → detect meaningful visual changes → suggest update → owner approves → digital pet model evolves.

Do not silently replace the pet's appearance.

Historical versions should remain part of the archive where possible.

---

## Memorial mode

If a real pet dies, the owner may choose to keep the digital companion in the house or move its memories into a dedicated memorial/archive state.

This must be optional and handled carefully.

FUL.HOUSE should never automatically infer that a pet has died from inactivity or old age.

Possible owner-controlled options:

- Keep the digital pet active in the house.
- Preserve the pet without further behavioral changes.
- Create a memory album/room/object.
- Keep the pet private.
- Include the pet in the inheritable family archive.

The product should avoid pretending that the digital pet is literally the deceased animal.

---

## Inheritance

Digitized real pets can become part of House inheritance.

If the owner has designated heirs, they may choose whether the pet's digital representation and archive transfer with the house.

This can preserve family memories of animals across generations.

Permissions must be explicit and configurable per pet.

---

## Generic pets remain useful

FUL.HOUSE should still offer generated/default pets:

- Cat
- Dog
- Bird
- Robot
- Fantasy creatures

These serve users without real pets and can support game mechanics.

But the premium emotional experience is:

**REAL PET → DIGITAL COMPANION**

---

## Product loop

Upload real pet → AI creates recognizable digital companion → pet inhabits house → behavior creates emotional activity → new real-life photos/videos update pet → memories accumulate → pet becomes part of House history.

---

## MVP

A realistic first version does not require perfect 3D reconstruction.

1. Upload 5–10 photos.
2. Enter name and short personality description.
3. Generate a stylized 2D/2.5D pet consistent with house art direction.
4. User approves or regenerates appearance.
5. Assign a small behavior profile.
6. Pet walks/sleeps/reacts using a library of predefined animations.
7. Butler references the pet in contextual events.

Later versions can use video for better appearance, motion signatures and 3D reconstruction.

---

## Key product principle

FUL.HOUSE should prefer **digitizing the user's real life** over asking the user to manufacture a fictional one.

LinkedIn can become the office.
Instagram can become memories and interests.
GitHub can become the workshop.
Real work can become products.
Real pets can become digital companions.

The house becomes valuable because it belongs specifically to this person and could not have been generated identically for anyone else.
